Good to know

Do I need a computer?No. An Android phone or tablet is enough. Whoply installs from the browser and opens like any other app.
Which languages does it work in?English, हिंदी and ગુજરાતી. Each person can pick their own language.
Does it need internet?Yes. Billing needs an internet connection today.
Can my staff use it?Yes. Each one gets a separate login, so nobody needs your password.
Is my data safe?Your data is backed up every day, and you can download your bills at any time.
What if I need help?There is a Help and Support chat inside the app, and we help you with the setup.
Stop running your store on paper. Ask us for a demo on your own phone. You will see your own items on your own bill in a few minutes.
